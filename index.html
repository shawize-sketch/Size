Your words.
Your choices.

  • Temporary by default. Messages, reflections, mood, and chosen context stay in this tab’s memory until you close or clear it. They are not saved automatically.
  • Optional encrypted notebook. If you choose to save, conversation and notes are encrypted with AES-GCM and a key derived from your passphrase. The passphrase is not saved. Locking clears the open conversation from the interface and unloads the AI.
  • Local chat processing. The AI runtime and model download from external providers when you load a model. Chat text is processed in your browser. Downloads and external resource visits still reveal normal network connection information to those providers.
  • Dictation is a separate choice. Your browser may use an online speech service. Wizship explains this before starting dictation. It never starts the microphone automatically.
  • Your downloads are your copies. Exported conversations and notes are plain-text files, readable to anyone who can open them. Nothing is sent to a partner or another person.
  • A shared device needs care. An open page can be read by someone using it. Encryption cannot protect against a compromised device, a malicious browser extension, or altered website code. A cover screen does not erase history.
  • No advertising trackers or chat analytics. Wizship has no account system, remote chat database, or hidden conversation uploads.

Encrypted notebooks lock after the page has been hidden for a minute. If saving is unavailable, the conversation remains temporary. Clearing this site’s browser storage removes saved notebooks and AI downloads.

    This space started with a friend wanting to make something good for a friend. A place to feel heard, think clearly, and take one thoughtful step.

    Wizship is an AI companion inspired by Wiz’s warmth. It is not Wiz himself, a therapist, or an emergency service. It can help you reflect, write, and consider options; it cannot know what someone else thinks or promise an outcome.

    Wizship brings the conversation. Xion brings perspective. Michael looks after the boundaries.

    These are coordinated roles around one local model and built-in checks. They help organize the work; they are not three separate people or independently trained models.
